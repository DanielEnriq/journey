Vowe · canvas building blocks

The parts Vowe draws a change with

Vowe never draws a generic box. For each view it picks blocks from this directory, binds them to what the system is actually doing, and arranges them for the question in front of the room. Shape says what a part is. What’s drawn inside says what is happening to it right now.

3

Arrangements

How Vowe lays parts out depends on the question being asked, not on a house diagram style.

TopologyWho talks to whom, and what you run versus what you don’t. Used for “what is this?”
SequenceOne job followed through time. Used for “what happens if…?”
Fork and convergeOne input, two copies, a shared outcome. Used for concurrency and races.
ComparisonOptions side by side under the part they would change. A set-aside option stays, struck through.
Evidence overlayThe topology the room already knows, with what was built laid on it and the checks beside it.

How the room’s views were composed

What Vowe picked for each question. A different system gets different blocks, the same rules.

Architecture“What is this?”Topology · Endpoint, Queue (jobs), Service (copies), External (calls), Store (rows) · Boundary around what you run
Failure flow“What if it crashes after the charge?”Sequence over job #4182 · Queue shows #4182 again · Worker crashed · Provider charged twice · Vowe points at step 3
Concurrency“What if two workers take the same job?”Fork and converge · Worker split into two copies · Queue shows #4182 held twice · both paths red into the provider
Alternatives“Lock, or idempotency?”Comparison under the worker · the lock struck through with its reopen condition · idempotency marked as in the decision
Provider timeout“What if the provider times out mid-charge?”Composed live by Vowe when Daniel asked about the provider · Sequence over job #4185 · Queue leased, Worker waiting, External with a charge whose answer was lost · steps placed by the composer, not by hand
Checkout states“What does the customer see while we retry?”Composed live · States from Pay, with Confirming proposed (dashed) · the PaymentStatus.tsx diff placed beside the states by the same composer
Implementation“Does what’s built match what we agreed?”Evidence overlay on the topology · new attempt_key column in the ledger · provider assumed until checked · checks from the signed decision
