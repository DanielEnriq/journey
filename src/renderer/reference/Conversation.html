Rules Vowe followsSet from the orb: “Chime in at pauses” (default) or “Only when asked”.
01Never starts talking while a person is talking. It waits, and says it is waiting.
02Holds one thought at a time. A second thought replaces the first rather than queueing.
03Stops on the word when talked over, and responds to the interruption rather than resuming.
04Doesn’t narrate small canvas changes. A change it makes gets one receipt with Undo, not a spoken explanation.
05Asks before acting on an ambiguous reference: “do you mean when it fails, or when it runs twice?”
06Never treats silence as agreement, and never invites a teammate without the room saying yes.
